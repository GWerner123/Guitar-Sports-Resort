Fretball

Pickleball you play with your guitar. Every note you play is a shot, and the matches teach you the fretboard as you go.

  • 🎸Your guitar. Any kind, as long as your phone can hear it.
  • 🎙Your phone’s microphone, near the guitar. The game asks for it when you start.
📱Turn your phone sideways to play
